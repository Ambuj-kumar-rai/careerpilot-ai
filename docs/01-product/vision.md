# CareerPilot AI

## Vision

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career platform designed to help
software engineers improve their resumes, understand ATS compatibility,
prepare for interviews, and create personalized career development plans.

## Initial Problem

Job seekers often do not know:

1. Why their resume is rejected.
2. Which skills are missing from their resume.
3. How closely their resume matches a job description.
4. How to improve their resume.
5. How to prepare for interviews.

## Initial Solution

CareerPilot AI will initially provide:

1. Resume upload
2. Resume parsing
3. ATS scoring
4. Job description matching
5. AI resume suggestions
6. AI resume rewriting

## Target Users

- Software engineers
- Fresh graduates
- Experienced developers
- Career switchers

## Initial MVP

Authentication
Resume Upload
Resume Parsing
ATS Analysis
AI Resume Review
Job Matching
