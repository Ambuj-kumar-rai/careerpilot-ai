# CareerPilot AI - Product Vision

## 1. Vision

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career platform designed to help people improve their resumes, understand how well their resumes match job requirements, identify skill gaps, prepare for career opportunities, and continuously improve their professional profiles.

The long-term vision is to build an intelligent career companion that helps users throughout their job-search and career-development journey.

CareerPilot AI should not simply tell a user:

> "Your resume score is 72."

It should explain:

> "Why is your score 72, what is missing, what can be improved, and what should you do next?"

---

# 2. The Problem

Job seekers often struggle to understand why their applications are unsuccessful.

Common problems include:

1. They do not know why their resume is rejected.
2. They do not understand ATS requirements.
3. They do not know which keywords are important for a particular job.
4. They do not know which skills are missing.
5. They struggle to write strong resume achievements.
6. They find it difficult to customize their resume for different jobs.
7. They do not know how closely their resume matches a job description.
8. They may receive generic resume advice that is not specific to their target job.
9. They do not have a clear career-improvement roadmap.
10. They spend significant time manually modifying resumes for different job applications.

# 12. AI Philosophy

AI should act as an assistant rather than replacing the user's judgment.

The platform should:

- Explain recommendations
- Provide actionable suggestions
- Avoid fabricating user experience
- Avoid encouraging users to claim skills they do not have
- Allow users to review AI-generated content
- Make recommendations understandable

The user remains in control of their resume and career decisions.

---

# 13. Trust and Transparency

CareerPilot AI should avoid presenting AI-generated scores as absolute truth.

For example:

```text
ATS Score: 82/100
```

should be accompanied by meaningful information explaining:

```text
Why the score is 82
What improved the score
What reduced the score
What the user can improve
```

The platform should clearly communicate that ATS behavior varies between employers, systems, job descriptions, and recruitment processes.

---

# 14. Product Principles

CareerPilot AI will follow these principles:

### User First

The product should solve real problems faced by job seekers.

### Actionable

Recommendations should tell users what they can actually do.

### Transparent

Users should understand why the system generated a recommendation.

### Honest

The system must not encourage users to misrepresent their experience.

### Secure

Personal and professional information must be protected.

### Modular

The platform should be designed so that new capabilities can be added without rewriting the entire system.

### Scalable

The architecture should support growth from an MVP to a larger platform.
